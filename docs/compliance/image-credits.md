## 1. Imagery Standards & Optimization Policy

All photographic and graphic assets utilized across XSignOn web properties conform to the following engineering standards:

1. **Next-Generation Formats**: Configured for dynamic AVIF and WebP delivery with automatic quality and device-width targeting.
2. **Accessible Text Equivalents**: 100% of photographic assets possess descriptive, contextual `alt` attributes conforming to WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.1.1 (Non-text Content). Purely decorative elements utilize empty `alt=""` or `aria-hidden="true"`.
3. **Clean Provenance**: All editorial and illustrative photography is sourced from licensed enterprise repositories with commercial re-use rights.
