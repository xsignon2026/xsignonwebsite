# XSignOn brief

Read this before writing. Update it when the company confirms a fact.

## Who XSignOn is (use only this)

- Cybersecurity and identity company. Based in McKinney, Texas, United States. Contact email on the live site: info@xsignon.com.
- Products: **PostureOne** (security posture across identity, governance, data and risk), **Passwordless Authenticators** (FIDO2 biometric hardware keys: AUTH Bio, AUTH Bio Pro, AUTH Bio Desktop), **EvalOne** (AI evaluation), **XSense** (identity analytics and MFA), **AMS** (asset management for the keys), **XSignOn Mesh** (micro-segmentation and containment; copy is a draft until the team confirms).
- Solution areas: Identity governance, AI and digital governance, Passwordless security, Quantum-safe security. Also on the live site: Business process solutions, Enterprise technology solutions.
- Standards the products are built toward (state only as "built to align with" until the team confirms the exact status): FIDO2, NIST AI-RMF, NIST 800-207 (zero trust), NIST post-quantum cryptography.

## The promise (one sentence, refine with the company)

> XSignOn helps organizations protect identity, govern risk and prepare for what comes next, with products for identity governance, passwordless sign-in, AI evaluation and emerging digital risk.

Every page should support this promise in its own way. If a sentence does not, cut it.

## Readers (write for these, in this order)

1. **Security and IT leaders** (CISO, head of IT, security architect): need evidence, scope and how it fits their stack. Technical product copy, grade 8 to 10.
2. **Executives and board members** (CEO, CFO, CXO): need risk, cost and decision clarity. Short, plain, outcome first, grade 6 to 8.
3. **Compliance and risk owners**: need what is covered, standards, evidence and audit trail.
4. **Buyers in regulated sectors** (healthcare, finance, government): need proof they can rely on, never hype.
5. **Smaller and mid-size organizations**: the live site says XSignOn fits mid-market and growth-stage firms, not only large enterprises. Do not assume a big security team.

Many readers are not native English speakers or read on a phone. Write plainly. Avoid idioms and slang.

## Voice (three traits, same on every page)

| Trait | Like this | Not like this |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | "Replace passwords with a fingerprint on a FIDO2 key." | "We empower organizations to unlock a seamless passwordless journey." |
| Calm | "Contain a breach before it spreads." | "Hackers are coming. Are you next?" |
| Precise | "Maps each AI deployment to NIST AI-RMF." | "World-class, best-in-class AI governance." |

Tone: confident and specific on marketing pages, patient and step-by-step on help pages, neutral and exact on legal pages. Tone never changes facts.

## Words to avoid in this domain

fear and hype: unhackable, bulletproof, 100% secure, military grade, foolproof, guaranteed protection, never breached, revolutionary, game-changing, next-generation, world-class, best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, holistic, end-to-end (unless literally true), AI-powered (unless the AI part is explained), "zero disruption" and "total coverage" (promises that cannot be proven).
Say what the product does and how you know.
