# Page patterns for xsignon.com

Section order on every content page: headline, supporting line, one primary CTA, proof strip, problem, solution, evidence, details, objections and FAQ, final CTA. Readers scan top to bottom; the most important sentence leads each section.

## Hero

Eyebrow (2 to 4 words), H1 of 6 to 12 words that states the outcome, a one or two sentence supporting line that adds the key detail or removes the main doubt, one primary CTA, optional secondary link, one proof line (only if verified).

> H1: [Outcome] for [audience], [key difference].
> Support: [How it works in one sentence]. [Doubt removed in one sentence].
> CTA: [Verb] [outcome].

## Home

Job: promise, proof, route. 400 to 800 words. H1 promise, supporting line, "Explore our products". Proof strip (verified facts only). Products (3 to 6 cards, one sentence each). The four capabilities. How we work in three steps. Standards the products align with (names only, per the claims file). Insights teaser. FAQ teaser. Final CTA.

## Product page

Job: what it is, who for, how it works, where to start. 600 to 1,200 words.
1. What it is: one paragraph of 40 to 60 words that stands alone.
2. Who it is for: 3 to 4 reader types.
3. Key features as "[Feature] so you can [benefit]" (3 to 6).
4. How it works: 3 to 5 numbered steps.
5. Fit with your stack: what it works with, how it deploys (SaaS, on-premises, air-gapped) only if true.
6. Evidence: an approved example or a measured result with source and date, else a clearly labelled scenario.
7. Specifications table (one fact per cell).
8. FAQ: 4 to 6 real questions.
9. CTA: "Contact us" or "Book a strategy call", with what happens next.

Passwordless key models: one short comparison table (model, standards, who it is for, key difference), then a section per model. Use only specs the team has confirmed.

## Solution page

Job: start from the problem. Heading as the problem in the reader's words, two or three sentences on what it costs them, then the approach in 3 to 5 steps, which XSignOn products deliver it (named, linked), one scenario, FAQ, CTA.

## About

Job: trust. Who we are (company statement and what we work on), what we do (four capabilities), how we work (the three stages with deliverables), what guides us (principles shown as actions), products connection (short, linked), careers banner, contact CTA. Add leadership and history only when confirmed; never invent them.

## Article (Insights)

800 to 2,000 words. Title as the question or the promise. Direct answer in the first two sentences (40 to 60 words). Key facts box (3 to 5 bullets). H2 questions with direct answers. Evidence with sources and dates. A worked example. "What to do next" steps. Author byline with role, published and updated dates, reviewed-by line. Related links. FAQ block (2 to 4). Single CTA to subscribe or contact. No emoji.

## Case study / proof

Only with written customer approval. Context, problem, what was done, outcome with numbers and method and period, a quote with name and role, what the reader can learn. If unapproved, use a clearly labelled example, never a fake customer.

## FAQ

Real questions in the reader's words. Answer in the first sentence, then detail, 30 to 80 words each. Order by how much each blocks action: price, timing, risk, fit, integration. Link to detail pages. Marked up with FAQPage schema that matches the visible text.

## Contact

150 to 300 words. Form or contact options, what happens next and when, response time (only if confirmed), what to include in the message, email alternative. Explain why each form field is needed.

## Careers

Who we hire, how we work, location (state only what is true; do not claim remote roles unless current roles support it), open roles with plain titles, how to apply, what happens after. If no roles: "There are no open positions right now. Follow XSignOn for future opportunities."

## Legal (Privacy, Terms)

Plain summary at the top (grade 8), full counsel-approved text below, last-updated date, contact for requests. The copywriter drafts the summary only.

## Microcopy

See `style-guide.md` for buttons, forms, errors, empty states, confirmations, 404 and cookie text.

## Footer

Same on every page: navigation repeat, contact email, social links with labels, privacy and terms, copyright with year.

## Drafting order for the site (do not write in random order)

1. Foundation: readers, promise, proof (verified), voice, sitemap, nav labels, keyword map, content inventory.
2. Home.
3. Main product pages.
4. About.
5. Proof and case studies.
6. Contact and FAQ.
7. Insights.
8. Legal summaries.
9. Microcopy and forms.
10. SEO and GEO layer, accessibility, claims, legal checks.
11. Publish with an owner and a review date per page; review core pages every 3 months.
